# Overview

This page defines exposure in the context of computational toxicology. Exposure refers to the contact of a living organism with a substance or agent.

## Scope and Notes

This page focuses on the definition and interpretation of exposure in toxicology. For detailed information on specific exposures, refer to the linked pages.

### Types of Exposure

Exposure can occur through various routes, including:

- **Inhalation**: Exposure through the respiratory system.
- **Ingestion**: Exposure through the digestive system.
- **Dermal Contact**: Exposure through the skin.
- **Ocular Contact**: Exposure through the eyes.
