# Overview

This page defines hazard in the context of computational toxicology. Hazard refers to the inherent property of a substance or agent to cause harm to living organisms or the environment.

## Scope and Notes

### Types of Hazard

Hazards can be categorized into several types, including:

- **Toxic Hazard**: The potential of a substance to cause adverse health effects.
- **Ecotoxicological Hazard**: The potential of a substance to cause harm to ecosystems.
- **Physical Hazard**: The potential of a substance to cause harm due to its physical properties (e.g., flammability, explosiveness).
