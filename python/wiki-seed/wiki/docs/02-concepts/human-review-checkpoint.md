## Overview

Human review checkpoints are critical points in the wiki's workflow where human intervention is required to ensure the accuracy, reliability, and quality of the content. These checkpoints are triggered by specific conditions or events, such as contradictions, unresolved claims, or significant updates.

## Scope and Notes

This page defines the conditions under which human review is triggered and the process for conducting human reviews. It does not cover the broader governance of human review, which is addressed in separate pages.

## Key Claims or Definitions

### Definition of a Human Review Checkpoint

A human review checkpoint is a point in the wiki's workflow where human intervention is required to:

1. Resolve contradictions between claims.
2. Verify claims that cannot be resolved autonomously.
3. Review significant updates or changes to the wiki.
4. Ensure compliance with quality standards.

### Conditions for Triggering Human Review

Human review is triggered under the following conditions:

1. **Contradictions**: When a claim is contradicted by another claim or source.
2. **Unresolved Claims**: When a claim cannot be verified or resolved autonomously.
3. **Significant Updates**: When a page undergoes major revisions or updates.
4. **Quality Flags**: When a page or claim is flagged for quality issues.
5. **Policy Changes**: When changes to governance policies affect existing content.

## Evidence or Details

### Process for Human Review

1. **Identify the Trigger**: Determine the condition that triggered the human review checkpoint.
2. **Notify Reviewers**: Alert the appropriate reviewers or subject matter experts.
3. **Review Content**: Conduct a thorough review of the content in question.
4. **Resolve Issues**: Address contradictions, verify claims, or update content as needed.
5. **Document Changes**: Record the changes made and the rationale for them.
6. **Update Status**: Mark the content as reviewed and update its status accordingly.

### Roles and Responsibilities

- **Reviewers**: Subject matter experts responsible for reviewing content and resolving issues.
- **Content Editors**: Responsible for updating content based on review feedback.
- **Governance Team**: Oversees the human review process and ensures compliance with policies.
