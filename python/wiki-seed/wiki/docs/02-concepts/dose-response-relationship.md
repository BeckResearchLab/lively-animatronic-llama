# Overview

This page defines the dose-response relationship in the context of computational toxicology. The dose-response relationship describes how the magnitude of an effect changes with varying doses of a substance.

## Scope and Notes

This page focuses on the definition and interpretation of the dose-response relationship in toxicology. For detailed information on specific dose-response relationships, refer to the linked pages.

### Components of Dose-Response Relationship

The dose-response relationship typically includes:

- **Dose**: The amount of substance administered or encountered.
- **Response**: The magnitude of the effect observed.
- **Threshold**: The dose at which the effect begins to be observed.
- **Slope**: The rate at which the effect increases with dose.
