# Ontology Crosswalks

This page provides ontology crosswalks used throughout the wiki. Ontology crosswalks map terms between different ontologies or vocabularies, ensuring consistency and interoperability.

## Overview

Ontology crosswalks are essential for integrating data from different sources and ensuring that terms are consistently interpreted across the wiki. This page lists the crosswalks used and provides brief descriptions of each.

## Common Ontology Crosswalks

### Gene Ontology (GO) to NCBI Taxonomy

- **Description**: Maps Gene Ontology terms to NCBI Taxonomy identifiers.
- **Example**: GO:0008150 (Biological Process) → NCBI Taxonomy: 9606 (Homo sapiens)
- **Use**: Used to link biological processes to species.

### Chemical Entities of Biological Interest (ChEBI) to CAS Registry Number

- **Description**: Maps ChEBI identifiers to CAS Registry Numbers.
- **Example**: ChEBI:16716 (Benzene) → CAS: 7440-44-0
- **Use**: Used to link chemical entities to their CAS identifiers.

### Medical Subject Headings (MeSH) to SNOMED CT

- **Description**: Maps MeSH terms to SNOMED CT codes.
- **Example**: MeSH: D001569 (Benzene) → SNOMED CT: 244372009
- **Use**: Used to link medical terms to clinical codes.

### Systematized Nomenclature of Medicine (SNOMED CT) to International Classification of Diseases (ICD)

- **Description**: Maps SNOMED CT codes to ICD codes.
- **Example**: SNOMED CT: 244372009 → ICD-10: T59.9 (Toxic effect of unspecified substance)
- **Use**: Used to link clinical terms to diagnostic codes.

### Ontology for Biomedical Investigations (OBI) to Experimental Factor Ontology (EFO)

- **Description**: Maps OBI terms to EFO terms.
- **Example**: OBI:0000257 (Assay) → EFO:0000324 (Assay)
- **Use**: Used to link experimental terms to assay terms.

## Custom Ontology Crosswalks

### Wiki-Specific Ontology Crosswalks

- **Description**: Custom crosswalks developed for the wiki to map terms between internal ontologies and external standards.
- **Example**: Wiki:Chemical → CAS: 7440-44-0
- **Use**: Used to ensure consistency between internal and external identifiers.

### Domain-Specific Ontology Crosswalks

- **Description**: Crosswalks developed for specific domains, such as toxicology or pharmacology.
- **Example**: Toxicology Ontology → Pharmacology Ontology
- **Use**: Used to link terms between related but distinct domains.

## Tools and Resources

### Ontology Lookup Service (OLS)

- **Description**: A web service that provides access to ontologies and their terms.
- **URL**: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols/index
- **Use**: Used to look up ontology terms and their mappings.

### BioPortal

- **Description**: A repository of biomedical ontologies and their mappings.
- **URL**: https://bioportal.bioontology.org/
- **Use**: Used to explore and download ontology crosswalks.

### Ontology Registry

- **Description**: A registry of ontologies and their crosswalks.
- **URL**: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols/registry
- **Use**: Used to discover and access ontology crosswalks.
