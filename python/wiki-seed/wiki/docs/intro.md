# Welcome to the Computational Toxicology Wiki

Last updated: 2026-08-19

This wiki serves as a knowledge substrate for toxicological research, evidence, and workflows. It is designed to support both human researchers and automated agents in navigating the complex landscape of computational toxicology.

## Mission and Scope

The mission of this wiki is to provide a structured, evidence-centered repository for toxicological data, concepts, and workflows. It aims to facilitate collaboration, reproducibility, and the integration of diverse data sources and methods.

## Key Features

- **Structured Knowledge**: Organized into categories such as chemicals, biology, toxicological endpoints, assays, datasets, and more.
- **Evidence-Centered**: Focuses on evidence claims, contradiction registers, and weight-of-evidence assessments.
- **Agent-Operable**: Designed to be accessible and usable by automated agents through structured front matter and consistent sections.
- **Workflows**: Includes templates and guidelines for literature review, hazard assessment, and dataset profiling.

## Getting Started

To begin exploring the wiki, use the sidebar to navigate through the categories. Each category contains subpages with detailed information and links to related resources.
