# Welcome to the Computational Toxicology Wiki

This wiki serves as the central knowledge substrate for the computational toxicology system, providing structured information on chemicals, biological targets, toxicological endpoints, assays, datasets, models, literature, evidence, workflows, and more.

## Purpose

The wiki is designed to support both human users and automated agents in navigating and contributing to the knowledge base. It adheres to a strict evidence-centered approach, ensuring that all claims are traceable, verifiable, and aligned with established scientific standards.

## Structure

The wiki is organized into the following categories:

- **System**: Overview of the computational toxicology system and agent roles.
- **Indices**: Master indices for chemicals, endpoints, assays, datasets, literature, and workflows.
- **Concepts**: Core concepts such as hazard, risk, exposure, QSAR, and adverse outcome pathways.
- **Chemicals**: Information on chemical entities, including substances, mixtures, and metabolites.
- **Biology**: Details on biological targets, pathways, and species.
- **Toxicological Endpoints**: Descriptions of toxicological endpoints and their relevance.
- **Assays**: Information on assays used to assess toxicological properties.
- **Datasets**: References to key datasets used in the system.
- **Models and Methods**: Descriptions of models and methods employed in the system.
- **Literature**: Curated literature, including papers, reviews, and regulatory reports.
- **Evidence**: Structured evidence claims and contradiction registers.
- **Workflows**: Standardized workflows for literature review, hazard assessment, and more.
- **Agent Operations**: Templates and records for agent tasks and operations.
- **Projects**: Index of active projects and their status.
- **Quality and Governance**: Standards, policies, and guidelines for evidence and responsible use.
- **Glossary**: Definitions of key terms and concepts.

## Getting Started

To begin exploring the wiki, use the sidebar to navigate to the category of interest. Each category contains a `_category_.json` file that provides an overview and links to relevant pages.

## Contributing

Contributions to the wiki are welcome. Ensure that all new content adheres to the evidence-centered approach and includes proper citations and provenance information.

## Support

For questions or issues, refer to the [Quality and Governance](14-quality-and-governance) section or contact the system administrators.
