# Known Limitations of the Wiki

This page documents the known limitations of the wiki. Awareness of these limitations helps users and contributors understand the scope and reliability of the content.

## Scope Limitations

1. **Coverage**: The wiki may not cover all chemicals, studies, or topics in the domain. Coverage is prioritized based on relevance and availability of data.

2. **Depth**: Some topics may be covered at a high level without detailed analysis or synthesis.

3. **Temporal Scope**: The wiki may not include the most recent studies or data due to delays in curation and review.

## Data Limitations

1. **Source Availability**: The wiki relies on publicly available sources. Proprietary or restricted data may not be included.

2. **Data Quality**: The quality of the content depends on the quality of the sources. Errors or biases in sources may be reflected in the wiki.

3. **Uncertainty**: Some claims may have high uncertainty due to limited or conflicting evidence.

## Technical Limitations

1. **Knowledge Representation**: The wiki's knowledge representation system may not capture all nuances or complexities of the domain.

2. **Interoperability**: Integration with external systems may be limited by technical constraints or lack of standards.

3. **Scalability**: The wiki's performance and usability may degrade as the volume of content grows.

## Mitigation Strategies

- **Coverage**: Prioritize high-impact topics and expand coverage incrementally.
- **Depth**: Provide links to external resources for deeper analysis.
- **Temporal Scope**: Implement regular updates to incorporate new data.
- **Data Quality**: Enforce strict evidence standards and review processes.
- **Uncertainty**: Clearly label claims with high uncertainty and provide context.
- **Technical Limitations**: Invest in improving the knowledge representation system and interoperability.
- **Scalability**: Optimize the wiki's architecture and implement caching or indexing strategies.
