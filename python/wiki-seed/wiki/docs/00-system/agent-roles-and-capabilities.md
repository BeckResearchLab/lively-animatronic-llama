# Overview

This page describes the roles and capabilities of agents within the computational toxicology system. Agents play a crucial role in automating tasks, ensuring data quality, and facilitating collaboration.

# Agent Roles

## Data Ingestion Agents

**Role**: Automate the ingestion of data from various sources, including literature, assays, datasets, and models.

**Capabilities**:
- Parse and extract structured data from unstructured sources.
- Validate and normalize data to ensure consistency.
- Integrate data into the wiki platform.

## Evidence Verification Agents

**Role**: Verify the accuracy and reliability of evidence presented in the wiki.

**Capabilities**:
- Cross-reference claims with cited sources.
- Identify contradictions or inconsistencies in the evidence.
- Flag claims that require human review.

## Knowledge Synthesis Agents

**Role**: Synthesize knowledge from diverse sources to provide comprehensive insights.

**Capabilities**:
- Analyze and summarize data from multiple sources.
- Generate structured evidence pages.
- Assist in the creation of concept and endpoint pages.

## Collaboration Agents

**Role**: Facilitate collaboration among researchers and stakeholders.

**Capabilities**:
- Provide automated responses to user queries.
- Assist in the creation and maintenance of workflows.
- Monitor and report on system performance and issues.

# Agent Capabilities

## Automation

Agents are designed to automate repetitive and time-consuming tasks, such as data ingestion, verification, and synthesis. This allows researchers to focus on more complex and creative aspects of their work.

## Data Quality Assurance

Agents ensure the quality and reliability of the data by validating sources, identifying inconsistencies, and flagging potential issues for human review.

## Collaboration Support

Agents facilitate collaboration by providing tools and processes for sharing information, coordinating tasks, and monitoring progress.
