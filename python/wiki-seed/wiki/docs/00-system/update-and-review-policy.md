# Update and Review Policy

This policy defines the procedures for updating and reviewing content in the wiki. It ensures that all content remains accurate, relevant, and up-to-date.

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish a structured approach for maintaining the integrity and reliability of the wiki's content.

## Scope

This policy applies to all content in the wiki, including claims, citations, and metadata. It covers the processes for proposing, reviewing, and implementing updates.

## Update Process

1. **Proposal**: Submit a proposal for an update, including the rationale and supporting evidence.

2. **Review**: The proposal is reviewed by the relevant stakeholders or a designated review team.

3. **Approval**: The proposal is approved, rejected, or requested for revision.

4. **Implementation**: Approved updates are implemented in the wiki.

5. **Verification**: Verify that the update has been correctly implemented and that the content is accurate.

## Review Process

- **Regular Reviews**: Conduct regular reviews of all content to ensure accuracy and relevance.
- **Ad-hoc Reviews**: Perform ad-hoc reviews in response to new evidence, corrections, or updates.
- **Review Team**: Assign a review team responsible for overseeing the review process.

## Documentation

- Document all updates, including the rationale, supporting evidence, and approval status.
- Maintain a log of all reviews, including the date, reviewer, and outcome.

## Examples

```yaml
update_id: upd-example-001

```

```yaml
review_id: rev-example-001
page_id: example-chemical
reviewed_by: "reviewer@example.com"
reviewed_on: 2026-08-19

```
