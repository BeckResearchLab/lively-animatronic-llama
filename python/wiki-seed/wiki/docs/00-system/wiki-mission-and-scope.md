# Overview

This page outlines the mission and scope of the wiki, providing a clear understanding of its purpose, goals, and boundaries.

# Mission

The mission of this wiki is to serve as a centralized, collaborative platform for documenting and synthesizing knowledge in computational toxicology. It aims to facilitate the sharing of information, evidence, and best practices among researchers, scientists, and stakeholders in the field.

# Scope

## In Scope

- **Computational Toxicology**: Focus on computational methods, models, and tools used in toxicology research.
- **Chemicals and Endpoints**: Documentation of chemicals, their properties, and associated toxicological endpoints.
- **Assays and Datasets**: Information on assays, datasets, and models relevant to toxicology.
- **Literature and Evidence**: Summaries and citations of literature sources and structured evidence.
- **Workflows and Governance**: Procedural guidelines, workflows, and governance policies for maintaining the wiki.

## Out of Scope

- **Clinical or Medical Advice**: The wiki does not provide clinical or medical advice.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: While it may reference regulatory standards, it does not ensure compliance.
- **Commercial or Proprietary Information**: The wiki does not include commercial or proprietary information unless explicitly permitted.

# Key Objectives

1. **Knowledge Synthesis**: Consolidate and synthesize knowledge from diverse sources to provide a comprehensive overview of computational toxicology.
2. **Collaboration**: Foster collaboration among researchers and stakeholders by providing a shared platform for information exchange.
3. **Quality Assurance**: Ensure the accuracy, reliability, and verifiability of the content through structured evidence and governance policies.
4. **Accessibility**: Make information accessible to a broad audience, including researchers, policymakers, and the general public.
