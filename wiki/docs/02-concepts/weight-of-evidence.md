# Overview

This page defines weight of evidence in the context of computational toxicology. Weight of evidence refers to the overall body of evidence supporting a particular conclusion or hypothesis.

## Scope and Notes

### Components of Weight of Evidence

Weight of evidence assessments typically include:

- **Consistency**: The degree to which multiple studies or sources agree.
- **Coherence**: The logical consistency of the evidence with established knowledge.
- **Temporal Relationship**: The temporal relationship between exposure and effect.
- **Strength of Association**: The strength of the association between exposure and effect.
