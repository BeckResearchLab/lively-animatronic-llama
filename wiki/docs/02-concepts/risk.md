# Overview

This page defines risk in the context of computational toxicology. Risk is the likelihood of adverse effects occurring under specific conditions of exposure.

### Components of Risk

Risk is typically composed of two main components:

- **Hazard**: The inherent property of a substance to cause harm.
- **Exposure**: The contact of a living organism with a substance or agent.

The relationship between hazard and exposure is often described by the dose-response relationship.
