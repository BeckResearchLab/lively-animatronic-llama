# Versioning and Audit Policy

This policy defines the procedures for versioning and auditing content in the wiki. It ensures that all changes are tracked, reviewed, and documented for transparency and accountability.

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish a structured approach for managing versions and auditing content in the wiki.

## Scope

This policy applies to all content in the wiki, including claims, citations, and metadata. It covers the processes for versioning, auditing, and documenting changes.

## Versioning Process

1. **Version Control**: Use a version control system to track changes to all content.

2. **Versioning Scheme**: Assign a unique version identifier to each update. Use a scheme such as semantic versioning (e.g., `1.0.0`).

3. **Change Log**: Maintain a change log for each page, documenting the version, date, author, and summary of changes.

4. **Rollback**: Provide a mechanism for rolling back to previous versions if necessary.

## Auditing Process

1. **Regular Audits**: Conduct regular audits of the wiki's content to ensure accuracy and compliance with policies.

2. **Ad-hoc Audits**: Perform ad-hoc audits in response to specific concerns or incidents.

3. **Audit Trail**: Maintain an audit trail for all changes, including the date, author, and rationale for the change.

4. **Review**: Assign a review team responsible for overseeing the auditing process.

## Documentation

- Document all versions, including the version identifier, date, author, and summary of changes.
- Maintain a log of all audits, including the date, auditor, and outcome.

## Examples

### Versioning Example

```yaml
version_id: "1.1.0"
page_id: example-chemical
changed_by: "editor@example.com"
changed_on: 2026-08-19
summary: "Updated toxicity claim with new data."
previous_version: "1.0.0"
```

### Audit Log Example

```yaml
audit_id: aud-example-001
audited_by: "auditor@example.com"
audited_on: 2026-08-19
outcome: "No issues found"
notes: "All content reviewed and found to be accurate and compliant."
```
