# Biosecurity and Dual-Use Considerations

This page documents the biosecurity and dual-use considerations for the wiki. It outlines the policies and procedures to ensure that the wiki's content does not pose risks to biosecurity or enable misuse.

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish guidelines for handling sensitive information and mitigating risks associated with dual-use research.

## Scope

This document applies to all content in the wiki that may have implications for biosecurity or dual-use. It covers the identification, assessment, and management of such risks.

## Risk Identification

1. **Dual-Use Research**: Identify content that could be used for both beneficial and harmful purposes.

2. **Sensitive Information**: Identify content that may pose risks to biosecurity, such as details on pathogenic agents or hazardous materials.

3. **Contextual Analysis**: Assess the context in which the information is presented to determine potential risks.

## Risk Assessment

1. **Risk Level**: Classify risks as low, medium, or high based on potential impact and likelihood.

2. **Impact Analysis**: Analyze the potential impact of misuse or unintended consequences.

3. **Likelihood Analysis**: Assess the likelihood of misuse or unintended consequences.

## Risk Mitigation

1. **Content Restriction**: Restrict access to or remove content that poses high risks.

2. **Contextual Warnings**: Provide warnings or disclaimers for content that may pose risks.

3. **Review and Approval**: Require review and approval for content that may pose risks.

4. **Education and Training**: Provide education and training for contributors and users on biosecurity and dual-use considerations.
