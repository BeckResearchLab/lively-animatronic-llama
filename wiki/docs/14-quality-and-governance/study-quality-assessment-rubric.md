# Study Quality Assessment Rubric

This rubric provides guidelines for assessing the quality of studies cited in the wiki. It ensures that only high-quality, reliable studies are used to support claims.

## Purpose

The purpose of this rubric is to establish a standardized approach for evaluating the quality of studies. It helps ensure that the wiki's content is based on reliable and valid evidence.

## Scope

This rubric applies to all studies cited in the wiki, including primary research, reviews, and meta-analyses. It covers the assessment of study design, methodology, and reporting.

## Assessment Criteria

### Study Design

1. **Appropriateness**: Is the study design appropriate for the research question?
   - **Excellent**: Study design is well-suited to the research question.
   - **Good**: Study design is generally appropriate but may have minor limitations.
   - **Fair**: Study design has notable limitations.
   - **Poor**: Study design is not appropriate for the research question.

2. **Rigor**: Is the study design rigorous and well-justified?
   - **Excellent**: Study design is rigorous and well-justified.
   - **Good**: Study design is generally rigorous but may lack some justification.
   - **Fair**: Study design has notable weaknesses.
   - **Poor**: Study design is not rigorous or justified.

### Methodology

1. **Sample Size**: Is the sample size adequate for the study's objectives?
   - **Excellent**: Sample size is adequate and well-justified.
   - **Good**: Sample size is generally adequate but may be slightly underpowered.
   - **Fair**: Sample size is inadequate for the study's objectives.
   - **Poor**: Sample size is severely inadequate.

2. **Data Collection**: Are the data collection methods appropriate and reliable?
   - **Excellent**: Data collection methods are appropriate and reliable.
   - **Good**: Data collection methods are generally appropriate but may have minor limitations.
   - **Fair**: Data collection methods have notable limitations.
   - **Poor**: Data collection methods are inappropriate or unreliable.

3. **Analysis**: Are the statistical or analytical methods appropriate and correctly applied?
   - **Excellent**: Statistical or analytical methods are appropriate and correctly applied.
   - **Good**: Statistical or analytical methods are generally appropriate but may have minor issues.
   - **Fair**: Statistical or analytical methods have notable issues.
   - **Poor**: Statistical or analytical methods are inappropriate or incorrectly applied.

### Reporting

1. **Transparency**: Is the reporting transparent and complete?
   - **Excellent**: Reporting is transparent and complete.
   - **Good**: Reporting is generally transparent but may lack some details.
   - **Fair**: Reporting has notable omissions or lack of transparency.
   - **Poor**: Reporting is incomplete or lacks transparency.

2. **Clarity**: Is the reporting clear and well-organized?
   - **Excellent**: Reporting is clear and well-organized.
   - **Good**: Reporting is generally clear but may have minor organizational issues.
   - **Fair**: Reporting has notable clarity or organizational issues.
   - **Poor**: Reporting is unclear or poorly organized.

## Scoring

- **Excellent**: 4 points
- **Good**: 3 points
- **Fair**: 2 points
- **Poor**: 1 point

### Overall Quality Score

- **9-10**: High quality
- **7-8**: Moderate quality
- **5-6**: Low quality
- **1-4**: Very low quality

## Examples

### Study Assessment Example

```yaml
assessment_id: ass-study-001
study_id: cit-example-001
assessed_on: 2026-08-19
assessed_by: "reviewer@example.com"

  data_collection: good
  analysis: excellent

  transparency: good
  clarity: excellent
overall_score: 9
quality_level: high
notes: "Study is of high quality with minor limitations in reporting transparency."
```
