## Overview

This paper presents a standardized approach to benchmark dose (BMD) modeling to improve consistency and transparency in human health assessments of chemicals. The authors address the lack of standardization in BMD methods and propose guidelines for better application, interpretation, and reporting.

## Scope and Source Notes

This is a primary technical paper focusing on methodological improvements in benchmark dose modeling. The paper addresses the inconsistency and lack of transparency in current BMD applications and proposes standardization guidelines. It compares BMD methods with traditional points of departure (PODs) such as NOAELs.
