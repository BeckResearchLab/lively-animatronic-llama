## Overview

This page contains the literature record for ECETOC Workshop Report 38, which explores best practices in building quantitative adverse outcome pathways (qAOPs). The workshop focused on practical guidance for developing, implementing, and reporting qAOPs in regulatory and research contexts.

## Workshop Content Summary

The workshop addressed several key aspects of qAOP development:

1. **Definition and Scope**: Clarified what constitutes a quantitative AOP and how it differs from traditional AOPs
2. **Model Types**: Discussed different approaches to quantitative modeling including semi-quantitative, probabilistic, and mechanistic models
3. **Data Requirements**: Identified the types and quality of data needed for robust qAOP development
4. **Implementation Strategies**: Provided practical guidance for applying qAOPs in regulatory and research contexts
5. **Case Studies**: Presented examples of successful qAOP applications and lessons learned
