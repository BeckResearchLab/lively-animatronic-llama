**Verification Status:** supported
**Confidence:** medium
**Verification Notes:** Supported by "Influence of BPA and its analogs on estrogen activity" section stating "The ability of BPA to bind ERα and ERβ is extremely weak... # Open Questions and Review Needs

- The review highlights the need for further studies on synergistic and additive effects of BPA and its analogs.
- The role of alternative mechanisms (e.g., membrane receptor binding, epigenetic changes) in endocrine disruption requires more investigation.
- The potential health implications of low-dose exposure to BPA analogs need to be clarified.
