## Overview

The European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) has specific requirements and guidance for read-across assessments in food and feed safety evaluations. This page provides detailed information on EFSA's approach to read-across, including methodological requirements, documentation standards, and assessment criteria.

## Scope and Notes

This page focuses specifically on EFSA's requirements for read-across assessments in the context of food and feed safety. It covers the regulatory remit, methodological expectations, documentation requirements, and assessment criteria that are unique to EFSA's framework.

## Key Definitions and Claims

> **Claim**: EFSA's remit includes risk assessment and regulatory advice on the safety of a broad range of substances and foodstuffs, such as novel foods, food and feed additives, and pesticides.

## EFSA's Regulatory Remit

### Substances and Products Covered

EFSA's read-across guidance applies to:

- **Novel Foods**: New or recently recognized food ingredients
- **Food Additives**: Substances added to food for technological purposes
- **Feed Additives**: Substances added to animal feed
- **Pesticides**: Plant protection products and their residues
- **Contaminants**: Unintentional substances in food and feed
- **Food Enzymes**: Enzymes used in food processing
- **Food Contact Materials**: Substances migrating from packaging

### Assessment Types

- **Safety Assessments**: Evaluation of potential health risks
- **Risk Assessments**: Characterization of risks under intended use
- **Exposure Assessments**: Evaluation of consumer exposure levels
- **Hazard Identification**: Identification of potential hazards
- **Hazard Characterization**: Dose-response assessment

## Workflow Adaptation for EFSA

### EFSA-Specific Workflow Steps

1. **Enhanced Problem Formulation**:
   - Food/feed-specific assessment goals
   - Consumer exposure considerations
   - Regulatory context definition

2. **Food-Specific Data Gap Analysis**:
   - Food/feed-relevant endpoints
   - Exposure data requirements
   - Consumer protection focus

3. **Mechanistic Similarity Assessment**:
   - Food/feed-relevant mechanisms
   - Pathway consistency evaluation
   - Biological plausibility verification

4. **NAM Integration**:
   - Strong encouragement of NAMs
   - Bioactivity data incorporation
   - In vitro assay integration

5. **Dedicated Uncertainty Analysis**:
   - Food/feed-specific uncertainties
   - Exposure-related uncertainties
   - Consumer protection factors

6. **EFSA-Compliant Documentation**:
   - Use of EFSA templates
   - Meeting reporting requirements
   - Framework-specific formatting
