## Overview

Read-across plays a crucial role in regulatory toxicology by enabling data-poor chemical assessments, supporting hazard classification, and facilitating regulatory compliance. This page explores the various regulatory applications of read-across methods across different frameworks and jurisdictions.

## Scope and Notes

This page covers the regulatory applications of read-across in chemical safety assessment, focusing on its role in hazard classification, data gap filling, risk assessment, and regulatory compliance. It addresses both general applications and framework-specific uses.

## Core Regulatory Applications

### Hazard Classification and Labeling

**CLP Regulation (EU)**:
- Supports hazard classification under Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008
- Contributes to weight-of-evidence assessments
- Enables classification of data-poor chemicals
- Facilitates appropriate labeling requirements

**GHS (Global Harmonization System)**:
- Supports international hazard classification
- Enables consistent labeling across jurisdictions
- Facilitates global chemical safety communication

### Data Gap Filling

**REACH Regulation (EU)**:
- Fills data gaps for chemical safety assessments
- Supports registration dossiers for data-poor substances
- Enables compliance with information requirements
- Reduces need for additional testing

**EFSA Food/Food Safety**:
- Fills gaps in toxicological data for food additives
- Supports safety assessments for novel foods
- Enables risk assessment for contaminants
- Facilitates regulatory decisions on food safety

### Risk Assessment

**Chemical Risk Assessment**:
- Supports hazard identification and characterization
- Enables dose-response assessment for data-poor chemicals
- Facilitates exposure assessment through similarity
- Contributes to risk characterization and management

**Food/Food Safety Risk Assessment**:
- Supports safety assessment of food additives
- Enables risk assessment for novel foods
- Facilitates assessment of pesticide residues
- Contributes to food contaminant risk assessment

### Regulatory Compliance

**Substance Registration**:
- Supports chemical registration under REACH
- Enables compliance with data requirements
- Facilitates dossier preparation
- Reduces regulatory burden for data-poor chemicals

**Product Authorization**:
- Supports authorization of biocidal products
- Enables safety assessment for plant protection products
- Facilitates regulatory approval of novel foods
- Contributes to cosmetic ingredient safety assessment

## Weight-of-Evidence Integration

### Weight-of-Evidence Principles

- **Data Integration**: Combines read-across with other data sources
- **Consistency Assessment**: Evaluates consistency across multiple data streams
- **Confidence Evaluation**: Assesses confidence in integrated evidence
- **Decision Support**: Provides basis for regulatory decisions

### Read-Across in Weight-of-Evidence

- **Supporting Evidence**: Read-across provides additional supporting evidence
- **Filling Gaps**: Addresses data gaps in weight-of-evidence assessments
- **Consistency Check**: Verifies consistency with other evidence streams
- **Confidence Builder**: Enhances confidence when supported by other data
