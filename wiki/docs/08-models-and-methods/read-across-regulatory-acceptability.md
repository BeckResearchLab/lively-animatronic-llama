## Overview

The regulatory acceptability of read-across assessments depends on multiple factors including scientific rigor, transparency, documentation quality, and alignment with specific regulatory frameworks. This page explores the key factors that influence regulatory acceptance and provides guidance for ensuring acceptable read-across submissions.

## Scope and Notes

This page focuses on the factors that determine whether read-across assessments will be accepted by regulatory authorities. It covers scientific, methodological, and procedural aspects that influence regulatory acceptance across different frameworks.

## Factors Influencing Regulatory Acceptability

### Scientific Rigor

**Chemical Similarity**:
- Structural similarity assessment
- Mechanistic similarity evaluation
- Physicochemical property comparison
- Toxicokinetic and toxicodynamic similarity

**Data Quality**:
- Quality of source chemical data
- Relevance of source data to target endpoints
- Reliability and reproducibility of data
- Compliance with data quality standards

**Methodological Soundness**:
- Appropriate choice of read-across method (analogue vs. category)
- Robust similarity assessment methodology
- Valid data transfer approach
- Proper uncertainty assessment

### Transparency and Documentation

**Clear Justification**:
- Transparent rationale for read-across approach
- Detailed similarity assessment documentation
- Clear data transfer justification
- Explicit uncertainty characterization

**Comprehensive Reporting**:
- Complete methodology description
- Detailed results presentation
- Thorough discussion of limitations
- Clear conclusions and recommendations

**Regulatory Compliance**:
- Framework-specific documentation requirements
- Compliance with reporting guidelines
- Adherence to submission formats
- Meeting regulatory deadlines

### Framework-Specific Requirements

**EFSA Food/Food Safety**:
- Mechanistic plausibility requirements
- NAM integration expectations
- Dedicated uncertainty analysis methods
- Food-specific endpoint considerations

**ECHA REACH**:
- RAAF framework compliance
- Scenario-based structure requirements
- Integrated uncertainty assessment
- Industrial chemical focus

**Other Frameworks**:
- Jurisdiction-specific requirements
- Framework preferences and limitations
- Cross-framework harmonization efforts

## Regulatory Context Variations

### Problem Formulation Impact

The problem formulation significantly influences regulatory acceptability:

- **Assessment Goal**: Clear definition of assessment purpose and endpoints
- **Regulatory Context**: Alignment with specific regulatory requirements
- **Data Availability**: Appropriate approach based on available data
- **Chemical Characteristics**: Suitability of read-across for the chemical type

### Framework Preferences

Different regulatory frameworks have varying preferences:

- **EFSA**: Strong emphasis on mechanistic plausibility and NAM integration
- **ECHA**: Preference for structured RAAF compliance
- **US EPA**: Focus on weight-of-evidence and data integration
- **Other Jurisdictions**: Varying requirements based on local regulations
