# Overview

Estrogen-related receptors (ERRs) are a group of nuclear receptors that are closely related to estrogen receptors but are not activated by estrogen. There are three main ERR isoforms: ERRα, ERRβ, and ERRγ. ERRs play crucial roles in energy metabolism, mitochondrial biogenesis, and cellular respiration. They are important targets for endocrine-disrupting chemicals (EDCs) such as BPA and its analogs that can interfere with their normal function.

# Receptor Isoforms

## ERR Alpha (ERRα)

- **Gene**: ESRRA
- **Location**: Chromosome 11 (humans)
- **Function**: Regulates mitochondrial biogenesis and oxidative metabolism
- **Expression**: High in heart, skeletal muscle, and brown adipose tissue
- **Toxicological Relevance**: Target for chemicals affecting energy metabolism

## ERR Beta (ERRβ)

- **Gene**: ESRRB
- **Location**: Chromosome 14 (humans)
- **Function**: Regulates neural development and energy homeostasis
- **Expression**: High in brain, placenta, and testis
- **Toxicological Relevance**: Important for developmental and neurological effects

## ERR Gamma (ERRγ)

- **Gene**: ESRRG
- **Location**: Chromosome 1 (humans)
- **Function**: Regulates mitochondrial function and cell proliferation
- **Expression**: High in placenta, pancreas, and cancer cells
- **Toxicological Relevance**: Important for tumor progression and cell proliferation

# Mechanisms of Action

## Classical Genomic Pathway

1. **DNA Binding**: ERR binds to estrogen response elements (EREs) in target gene promoters
2. **Heterodimerization**: ERR forms heterodimer with RXR (retinoid X receptor)
3. **Transcription Regulation**: Recruits coactivators to regulate gene expression
4. **Target Genes**: Mitochondrial genes, metabolic enzymes, cell cycle regulators

## Constitutive Activity

- **Ligand-Independent**: ERRs are constitutively active even without ligand binding
- **Inverse Agonists**: Some chemicals can inhibit ERR activity
- **Modulators**: Endocrine disruptors can modulate ERR activity through cofactor interactions

# Mechanisms of Endocrine Disruption

## BPA and BPA Analogs

BPA and its analogs can interfere with ERR signaling through:

- **Cofactor Interactions**: Displace coactivators or recruit corepressors
- **Transcription Regulation**: Modify gene expression patterns
- **Mitochondrial Function**: Alter mitochondrial biogenesis and respiration
- **Cell Proliferation**: Affect cell cycle regulation and tumor progression

## Specific Isoform Effects

- **ERRα**: Effects on energy metabolism and mitochondrial function
- **ERRβ**: Effects on neural development and energy homeostasis
- **ERRγ**: Effects on cell proliferation and tumor progression
- **Species Differences**: Vary in their ERR interaction profiles

# Toxicological Implications

## Health Effects

ERR disruption contributes to:

- **Metabolic System**: Obesity, diabetes, metabolic syndrome
- **Cardiovascular System**: Heart function and energy metabolism
- **Neurological System**: Neural development and cognitive function
- **Cancer**: Tumor progression and cell proliferation
- **Developmental Effects**: Fetal programming of metabolic disorders

## Mechanistic Effects

- **Mitochondrial Function**: Changes in mitochondrial biogenesis and respiration
- **Energy Metabolism**: Alterations in glucose and lipid metabolism
- **Cell Proliferation**: Effects on cell cycle regulation and apoptosis
- **Tumor Progression**: Modulation of cancer cell growth and metastasis

# Species-Specific Effects

- **Receptor Sequence**: ERR sequence varies between species
- **Gene Regulation**: Species-specific patterns of target gene activation
- **Toxicological Outcomes**: Vary significantly between species
- **Regulatory Implications**: Differences in sensitivity to endocrine disruptors
