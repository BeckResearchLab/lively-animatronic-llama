# Internationalization of Read-Across as a Validated New Approach Method (NAM) for Regulatory Toxicology

## Summary

This review paper examines the state of read-across (RAx) as a validated new approach methodology (NAM) for regulatory toxicology, focusing on its internationalization, validation frameworks, integration with other approaches, and regulatory acceptance. The paper provides a comprehensive overview of RAx principles, similarity assessment methodologies, NAM integration, adverse outcome pathways (AOPs), ADME considerations, validation requirements, and sector-specific applications.

## Key Topics Covered

1. **Read-Across Fundamentals**: Core principles and regulatory acceptance of RAx
2. **Similarity Assessment**: Methodologies for chemical and biological similarity
3. **New Approach Methodologies (NAMs)**: Integration with RAx for data generation
4. **Adverse Outcome Pathways (AOPs)**: Framework for mechanistic comparison
5. **ADME Considerations**: Absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion profiling
6. **Validation and Uncertainty**: Validation frameworks and uncertainty assessment
7. **Regulatory Frameworks**: ECHA, REACH, OECD, and other regulatory contexts
8. **Sector-Specific Applications**: Petrochemicals, fragrances, nanomaterials, medical devices
